A Celebration of Colours & Materials

With Noir Artistry, colour coating aside and a rare use of digital colouring on gold. Traditionally known as monochromatic, our gold pieces feature the intricate coloured patterns. What’s more, our charms also come with various enchanting textures, like mother of pearl or ceramics.

Silicone Lining for Easy Adjustment

The interior of each Charme is lined with high-grade, non-slip silicone using a processing technology; your Charme stays put no matter where you place it on the leather bracelet.

Durable, Safe and Eco-friendly Design

Charme, made with the gold electroforming technique, delivers unparalleled gold purity, durability and versatility. Producing at a higher cost, we ensure our trusted products are safe to our health and our environment.

Handcrafted by Dedicated Jewellers
